I'm Loïc Chagnoleau, an embedded software engineer based in France. I'm passionate about technology and how it can be used to solve real-world problems, and I enjoy the parts of software that sit closest to the hardware.

I care about clean, maintainable and scalable code — even (especially) when it has to fit in a few kilobytes of flash. I'm also a big fan of open-source software and try to give back to the community whenever I can.

## What I work on

- **Embedded firmware** — C on bare-metal ARM Cortex-M microcontrollers (STM32 and friends)
- **Low-level programming** — startup code, linker scripts, peripheral drivers, register-level work
- **Debugging & reverse engineering** — understanding systems from the binary, the datasheet or the bus
- **Tooling** — build systems, toolchains and developer workflows for embedded projects

## Work with me

I'm open to missions and collaborations on embedded and low-level projects. Whether you need help bringing up a new board, tracking down a hard-to-reproduce bug or reviewing firmware, feel free to reach out.
